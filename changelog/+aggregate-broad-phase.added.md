Add the experimental `"aggregate"` broad phase, `newton.geometry.BroadPhaseAggregate`, selected with `CollisionPipeline(model, broad_phase="aggregate")`. It bounds each articulation, or each body outside one, with one union box, finds overlapping boxes with a sweep and prune or a uniform grid, and tests their member shapes per warp; finite static shapes are binned once into a static grid, which `CollisionPipeline.refresh_static_shapes()` rebuilds after they move. `CollisionPipeline.broad_phase_statistics()` reports the candidate-pair count and capacity in every mode, plus the aggregate mode's demand, overflow and grid statistics.
