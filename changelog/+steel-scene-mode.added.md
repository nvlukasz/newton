Add the experimental `solve_mode="scene"` to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`, which folds every world of the model into one solve world: its rows form units (an articulation's own rows, a contact), the units that join two articulations are colored every step and solved color by color, and the row budgets are pooled over the worlds, each unit kept or dropped whole (they stay per world, so one world holding many articulations needs `articulation_constraint_max` scaled with their number). `scene_phase_order` sets the order of the two phases of each iteration and `scene_unit_max` the unit capacity; `SolverSteel.scene_statistics()`, `SolverSteel.color_statistics()` and `SolverSteel.time_scene_units()` read the rows, units and colors back. The mode rejects `nesterov_momentum`, `reuse_kinematics` and coupling, solves `articulated_contact_response="propagation"` with the immediate response, and runs on the CPU wherever Steel does.
