Add the experimental `solve_mode="scene"` to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`, which folds every world of the model into one solve world: its rows form units (an articulation's own rows, a contact), the units that join two articulations are colored every step and solved color by color, and the row budgets are pooled over the worlds, each unit kept or dropped whole. `scene_phase_order` sets the order of the two phases of each iteration and `scene_unit_max` the unit capacity; `SolverSteel.scene_statistics()`, `SolverSteel.color_statistics()` and `SolverSteel.time_scene_units()` read the rows, units and colors back. The mode rejects `nesterov_momentum`, `reuse_kinematics` and coupling, solves `articulated_contact_response="propagation"` with the immediate response, and runs on the CPU wherever Steel does.
